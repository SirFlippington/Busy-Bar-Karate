# Asset provenance

Karate uses the character sprites, background and audio supplied by Kenny Morgan during development. Kenny Morgan confirmed permission to redistribute these assets for the BUSY Apps Catalog submission on 24 November 2026. This statement records the author confirmation; it is not a claim that the assets are public domain because of their age.
